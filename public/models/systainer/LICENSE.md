# Systainer reference model

`sys-tloc.bin` is a re-encoded copy of the Festool T-Loc Systainers (SYS 1–SYS 5, full size
series) from "Festool T-Loc Systainers Reference Models" (https://www.printables.com/model/237425),
shared on Printables from the GrabCAD model by Ben
(https://grabcad.com/library/festool-systainers-sys-t-loc-1).

It is licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0**
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/), not under the AGPL that covers the rest of
Forma Studio. Changes: welded, re-oriented to the viewer's frame and quantised by
`scripts/build-systainer-model.mjs`, at full resolution (the parts ship as exported, ~370k
triangles total; the lid's Festool/Tanos lettering is pressed flat and the Reinforcement_Bar part
is skipped — it sits inside the front wall channel and never shows).
